# Satwik Kukadolli — Portfolio Brand

## Positioning

Satwik is a Forward-Deployed × GTM Engineer who turns ambiguous customer and revenue problems into production systems. He works across discovery, backend architecture, workflow automation, product delivery, and adoption.

## Primary Message

I turn customer friction into systems that ship, scale, and create growth.

## Supporting Messages

- Embed with the workflow before proposing the software.
- Build the API, automation, interface, and operating path together.
- Measure adoption and operational impact, not feature volume.
- Turn repeated field problems into reusable product and GTM patterns.

## Voice

- Confident, not inflated.
- Technical, not jargon-heavy.
- Commercially aware, not salesy.
- Direct, not cold.
- Curious, not vague.

## Proof Rules

- Use verified résumé metrics only.
- Label personal projects as personal projects.
- Label proposed GTM systems as representative blueprints.
- Never imply a client, adoption number, or outcome that is not documented.
- Distinguish shipped capabilities from actively developing skills.

## Visual Direction

The visual idea is “Field Notes from the Revenue Edge”: a dark editorial canvas where technical diagrams, deployment signals, and journey chapters behave like a living operating system.

- Deep ink surfaces create cinematic depth.
- Electric chartreuse signals action and activation.
- Cool cyan marks data, systems, and feedback.
- Warm paper tones keep the experience human.
- Large serif statements carry the story; precise sans and mono text carry evidence.

## Motion Direction

- Motion reveals cause and effect: signal → decision → system → outcome.
- One sticky narrative section provides the main scroll choreography.
- Decorative layers use subtle parallax; body copy never parallax-scrolls.
- Hover states expose architecture and evidence rather than adding noise.
- Reduced-motion mode presents the complete static state immediately.
